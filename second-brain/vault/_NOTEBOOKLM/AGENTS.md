# _NOTEBOOKLM/

**What it is:** optional study packs - pre-written prompts that turn a research artifact and its study record into external study material (audio overviews, guided quizzes).

**What lives here:** one pack per artifact under study, sharing the artifact's `YYYY-MM-DD-slug` stem.

**Fed by:** `_RESEARCH/` and `LEARNING/`. When the source pair merges or retires, the pack moves to `__archive/` with it.

## Standards

- The artifact, its study record and its pack share one stem and move together.
- Prompts stay generic and self-contained and include no external account details.
