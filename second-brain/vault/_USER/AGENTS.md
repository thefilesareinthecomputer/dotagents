# _USER/

**What it is:** the owner's personal ledger - what the owner has actually worked through, as opposed to what they have collected.

**What lives here:** `user-knowledge-profile.md`, an append-only table.

## Standards

- Append-only; rows are never edited or removed.
- The ledger is per user: the sample vault ships only this folder's docs, and the ledger itself is gitignored.
