# __archive/

**What it is:** where everything superseded goes - the vault's guarantee that nothing is ever deleted. Excluded from the knowledge graph (`.kgignore`) so stale copies never compete with live notes.

**What lives here:** superseded knowledge-base notes, merged study-pack trios (artifact + cert + pack), retired templates, closed project material.

**Fed by:** every folder in the vault.

**Feeds:** nothing - archived material is history, recoverable but inert.

## Standards

- Move whole files; never paste fragments or rewrite on the way in.
- Keep the original filename; on collision, prefix the move date.
- Never edit archived files; corrections happen in the living tiers.
