# About the Archive

Nothing is deleted from this vault. Superseded notes, merged study-pack trios, and retired project material move here instead, keeping history recoverable.

This folder is excluded from the knowledge graph (see `.kgignore` at the vault root) so stale copies never compete with live notes in search.
