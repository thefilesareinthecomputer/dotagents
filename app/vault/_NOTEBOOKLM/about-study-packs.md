# About Study Packs

This folder holds optional study-pack prompts: pre-written prompts that turn a research artifact and its certification into external study material, for example an audio overview or a guided quiz in a tool like NotebookLM or locally generated training materials.

Study packs are optional. The lifecycle does not depend on them; they exist to make material more accessible to auditory learners. When a pack's source material is merged or archived, the pack moves to `__archive/` with it.
