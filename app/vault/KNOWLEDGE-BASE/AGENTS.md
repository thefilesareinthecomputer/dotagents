# KNOWLEDGE-BASE/

**What it is:** the settled notes - the folder that counts as knowledge, as opposed to research still pending review.

**What lives here:** flat `DOMAIN-SUBJECT.md` notes, no subfolders.

**Feeds:** `PROJECTS/`, which draw on it. Superseded notes move to `__archive/`.

## Standards

- A note moves here once it is reviewed and settled; until then it waits in `_RESEARCH/`.
- A correction supersedes the note, and the old version moves to `__archive/`.
- Naming is flat: the domain prefix groups notes, and there are no subfolders.
