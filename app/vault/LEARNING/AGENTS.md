# LEARNING/

**What it is:** study records - what was studied from a research artifact, and how it went.

**What lives here:** one record per research artifact, sharing its `YYYY-MM-DD-slug` stem, with an append-only attempts table.

**Fed by:** `_RESEARCH/`. Retired records move to `__archive/` with their artifact.

## Standards

- Questions pair a principle with a use case and are never yes/no or multiple choice.
- Attempts are append-only, and history is never rewritten.
