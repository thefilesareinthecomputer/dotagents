# 2026-06-15 Season Review

Attendees reviewed water usage for the first half of the season.

Following the heat advisory, the group agreed that the watering interval is 5 days to conserve tank reserves, superseding the schedule in the [Irrigation Schedule Runbook](/knowledge-base/runbooks-irrigation-schedule.md).

Zone 3 coverage gaps were noted; drip line spacing there will be reduced next month.
