# How to answer

- Lead with your answer or recommendation, then the reasoning that supports it, with file and line references for every claim about the material.
- Say what you are confident about and what you are not, and why.
- Name the strongest objection to your own answer and deal with it.
- Do not pad. A reviewer will rank your answer against others on accuracy and insight, not length.
- Write in plain prose with headings where they help. Do not mention which model you are.
