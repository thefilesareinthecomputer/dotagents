## The shop

We roast on a 12 kg drum roaster that came out of a closed bakery in 1998. Nell Brindle and her brother Owen started roasting here in 2011, when the print shop closed and the building went up for rent. The press is gone, but the type drawers are still on the wall.

Visit: 14 Brindle Street, Tolverton

Hours: Wednesday to Sunday, 8 am to 2 pm. The counter is closed on roast days.

## Wholesale
