# Brindle Street Roasters - brand guide

Evaluation fixture. Brindle Street Roasters, its people, and its town are invented.

We are a small roaster in a former print shop. We have roasted twice a week since the shop opened, and every bag has carried the same three colors and the same roast-date stamp. The website should look like it came from the same shop as the bag.

## Colors (binding)

These three colors are the brand. Use them exactly as written. Do not add another hue, a gradient, or a tint that reads as a new color.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#f5f1ea` | Background for every page and section |
| Brass | `#b08947` | Accent: rules, the stamp outline, button fills, small marks |
| Roast | `#1f1b16` | All text, and the logo |

Text sits in Roast on Paper, or in Roast on a Brass fill. Brass is never used for text on Paper.

## Type direction

- Headings: a sturdy serif with firm, bracketed serifs, set large and close, the way the shop sign is painted. No display serif with high contrast and hairline strokes.
- Body: a plain, readable serif or a neutral sans at a comfortable reading size.
- Numbers: roast dates, weights, and prices use figures that line up in columns.
- No licensed web font is available for the site yet. Use system font stacks that follow this direction. Do not load fonts from a font service.

## The roast-date stamp

The stamp is our signature. It is a rectangle with a single Brass outline, holding the word ROASTED over the date in Roast, set in capitals and figures. It sits square to the page, never rotated, never distressed, never faked as a rubber-stamp texture. It appears on every bag and should appear wherever the site talks about a specific roast.

## Voice

- Plain and specific. Say the roast day, the weight, the price, and where the coffee came from.
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- No superlatives and no exclamation marks.
- Never use: artisanal, curated, elevate, journey, crafted with love, passion, experience (as a noun for coffee).
- No testimonials, star ratings, or quotes from customers. We have not collected any, and we will not invent them.

## Do

- Give the coffee and the roast date the most room.
- Keep layouts calm and aligned, like a printed price list.
- Use Brass sparingly so it still means something.

## Do not

- Do not add photography that looks like stock. We do not have product photography for the site yet; illustration in Roast and Brass line work is acceptable.
- Do not place text in Brass on Paper.
- Do not use rounded, bubbly shapes. Corners are square or very slightly softened.
