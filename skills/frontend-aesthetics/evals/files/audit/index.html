Walk the Merrow ridgeline this autumn

Six guided walks from the village hall, every Saturday from October 4 to November 8. Every walk is led by two volunteers and costs nothing for members.

Guided walks

Each walk starts from the village hall parking lot at 9:30 am and returns by early afternoon. Book a place so the leaders know how many to expect; walks go ahead in light rain and are canceled by 8 am if the forecast turns severe.

Membership

Adult membership is renewed once a year, and anyone under 18 joins free. Members walk for free, get the printed route cards, and vote at the spring meeting. Non-members are welcome on any walk for a small donation on the day.
