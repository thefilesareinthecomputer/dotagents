# USER-STORIES-RESEARCH

Research done for a story on [[user-stories]] before it can be built or answered. Each entry names its story, the question, the sources checked and their dates, and labels every finding **confirmed** (quoted from a primary source), **reported** (a community or field source), **inferred** or **not found**. A newer entry on a topic supersedes the earlier ones.

A finished entry leaves this file: its confirmed findings go to [[notes-learnings]] under the day's sweep, and anything still unanswered goes to [[notes-questions]].

> [!note] Entry format
> One H2 per question, `## YYYY-MM-DD-STORY-<id>-<TOPIC>`, newest first.

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD-STORY-<id>-TOPIC

- **Question** <what the story needs to know>
- **Sources** <page or document> (accessed YYYY-MM-DD)

### FINDINGS
- **confirmed** <the finding> - <source>
- **not found** <what was looked for, and where>
```
