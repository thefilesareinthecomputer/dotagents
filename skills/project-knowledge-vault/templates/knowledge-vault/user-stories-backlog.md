[BLOCKED](#BLOCKED)

The working backlog behind [[user-stories]]: follow-ups a closed story left behind, work nobody has opened an item for yet, and items set aside on purpose. An item moves to the live board when someone opens it there, and the entry here is removed in the same edit.

> [!note] Entry format
> One H3 per item, under the section that says why it is not on the board. Name the story it came from, what it is waiting for, and since when. A parked item says until when, or what would bring it back.

```markdown
### <STORY-id or a short title>

- **From** <the story or the conversation it came from> - **Since** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Waiting for** <what, and from whom>

<what the work is>
```

Work that cannot start until something outside the team changes.

Work that is ready to open on the board but has no item yet.
