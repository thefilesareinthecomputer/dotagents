# EMAIL-NOTES
[YYYY-MONTH](#YYYY-MONTH)

Email worth keeping, newest first, one section per exchange. Chat goes to [[notes-general-notes]].

Record what the exchange settled or asked for. Quote verbatim only where the exact wording is the thing that matters, such as a commitment or an approval.

> [!note] Entry format
> One H2 per exchange, `## YYYY-MM-DD` or `## YYYY-MM-DD-<TOPIC>`, dated by the message that opened it. Each month opens with a `# YYYY-MONTH` H1 and gets a jump link at the top of the file. Every subsection is optional.

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD-TOPIC

- **From** <ROLE> - **To** <ROLE or group>

<what was asked, and what came back>

### DECISIONS
- YYYY-MM-DD - <what was settled> - supersedes YYYY-MM-DD <ref>, <what changed>

### ACTIONS
- [ ] <action> (owner: <ROLE>; opened YYYY-MM-DD)
```
