# CHAT-NOTES

Chat and email in one file, newest first. One H2 per exchange, `## YYYY-MM-DD-TOPIC-SCOPE`, where scope names the audience.

Internal and external threads share this file on purpose. Splitting by audience or by channel breaks the sequence, and the sequence is what makes a later message readable as superseding an earlier one.

## YYYY-MM-DD-TOPIC-INTERNAL

What was said and what it settled. Paraphrase to the point rather than pasting the transcript, and quote verbatim only where the exact wording is the thing that matters.

- YYYY-MM-DD - <what was settled> - supersedes YYYY-MM-DD <ref>, <what changed>
